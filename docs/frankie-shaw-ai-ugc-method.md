# Frankie Shaw (@frankyecom) — AI UGC Method

**A creative-philosophy reference, not a generation technique.** Everything else in `docs/`
(`multi-image-role-tagging.md`, `seedance-comfy-handoff.md`, `forcing-the-cgi-look.md`) answers
*how to get a model to render X*. This answers a different question: *what should X even be* —
the concept, structure, and performance direction that makes an AI UGC ad actually work as an ad,
before a single prompt gets written. Read this before drafting a script or shot list for any
UGC-style content in this repo (currently: `clients/rocka-moss/ai-ugc-playbook.md`,
`prompts/selena-photoreal.md`, and the parked `clients/ai-ugc-agency/`).

## Who he is
D2C-native AI UGC ad maker. Also runs his own D2C brands (FRANKY scrub, ACV powder) — he eats his
own dog food. Sells a "framework" (V1 through V5.2), evolving from a $97 prompting/editing course
into a full GPT prompt-engine (~54k characters) built on Fable 5.

## His core philosophy
- **Realism is the entire product.** "You cannot tell this is AI" is the whole value prop —
  anything uncanny-valley gets killed. Gut check: *"If it looks planned, it failed. If it looks
  accidentally captured, it passed."*
- **Direction before dialogue.** Build in this order: environment → tonality/cadence → script.
  Environment carries more meaning than what's said.
- **Curiosity sells, pitches don't.** Structure is always curiosity → narrative → product,
  product last, close is soft ("look into it," never "shop now"). Before/after is dead (platforms
  reject it) — show progression instead. No bold/medical claims.
- **Speed and volume are the moat**, not any single clip. Repeated numbers: an ad in 4–30
  minutes, $2–10 in tokens, 100 creatives/week, ~16 base clips → 50+ variants via speed-mapping to
  viral audio in CapCut.
- **Honest caveat he repeats constantly:** "quick Seedance clips don't print money" — realism
  comes from deep prompting/direction/storytelling, the tool is not the moat by itself.
- **Consumer psychology is the deeper layer (V5.2):** subconscious buying behavior, attention
  manipulation, TOF psychology, conversion triggers — tailored per archetype (5 motivation
  buckets: avoid pain, gain confidence, status/envy, love & belonging, convenience/peace).

## Selling structure
- Curiosity → narrative → product. Product comes last, never first.
- No hard sell. The "self-found" close = "look into it," never "shop now."
- Before/after is dead (ad platforms reject it) — show progression/momentum instead.
- No bold or medical claims — persuade with story, not claims.

**Transformational-product formula (5 beats):**
1. Relatable scenario (put their life on screen)
2. Character building (show the improvable lifestyle — no product yet)
3. Show transformation/progress (not before-after)
4. Introduce product (now it makes sense)
5. Grand reveal (curiosity → click)

## The format taxonomy (ranked by what actually hits)
1. **Engagement-bait product placement** — his biggest hits; product is incidental, never sold
   (yearbook flip 2.4M views, nightclub tally game 900K, blind smell-test, fake reunion, animal
   moments, surreal mascot). Drives comments + mass awareness, not direct sales.
2. **Podcast reaction/authority ads** — fake-branded shows ("The Talk," "GIRLMIND," "SURF
   STORIES"), cast as an affluent grey-haired man or young woman at a mic, understated reaction
   lines ("Wait — that's it?"). One of his biggest categories; "infinite length podcast clips" is
   a v5.1 signature — positioned as an $8K-shoot replacement done in ~25 minutes.
3. **In-car confessional** — front camera, seatbelt on, nonchalant "okay this just happened"
   recap after a store interaction. Good for men <30 (cologne) and busy-person credibility plays
   (e.g., a nurse recommending a supplement). Distinct from the podcast format but shares the same
   "caught candidly" realism logic.
4. **Street interview** — mic in hand, "what are you wearing/drinking," compliment-stop as the
   hook.
5. **Founders/origin story** — heartwarming, handmade, "SINCE... TO..." arc.
6. **Mirror selfie** — get-ready/outfit-check energy, skincare or apparel.
7. **Keynote/authority** — founder on a conference stage, filmed from the crowd.
8. **Home/lifestyle** — clean aspirational domestic (bedding, gadgets).
9. **Skincare realism** — visible imperfections (acne, pores, freckles) left in as the flex, no
   smoothing.
10. **Fitness/physique** — gym or outdoors, understated "what's your secret."
11. **Lead-gen/advertorial** — heavy emotion + proof-in-hand (settlement check, utility bill),
    non-transactional "coming to you as a friend" tone.

## Hooks (first 1–3 seconds, always curiosity-first)
| Hook pattern | Examples |
|---|---|
| Compliment-stop | "wait, you smell amazing — what are you wearing?" |
| "Okay, this just happened" (in-car recap) | "I literally just chased a girl across the store because she smelled so good…" |
| "Be honest…" challenge | "be honest — double chin, fat, or what?" |
| "People always ask how I…" | "people always ask how I live like this. it's not crypto…" |
| Transformation/progression | "this was me a year ago before my alkalizing journey…" |
| Defensive "not sponsored" (older skeptics) | "I'm not sponsored or any of that BS… look into it." |
| Casual reveal ("did I tell you…") | "did I tell you about the gummy that finally made my hair grow…" |
| Bold claim / Pinocchio paradox (men <30) | "whatever's in this pheromone cologne is making people freak out…" |
| The gatekeep | "this $30 perfume — stop gatekeeping. I'm gatekeeping." |
| Proof-in-hand (lead gen) | "every lawyer told me I didn't have a case, now I'm holding $97,000…" |
| The tally/game (engagement bait) | "which server does the most in bottle service? 🤔" |

**Close, always soft:** "look into it," "just look into it," or curiosity that sends them to the
bio/link — never "shop now."

## Consumer archetypes
| Archetype | What works |
|---|---|
| **Women 50+** | Respect experience, function over hype, natural over vanity, passive, avoid bold claims, "self-found" |
| **Women <30** | Blends into feed, story/scenario sells, inexpensive mass-appeal products, raw relatable emotion |
| **Men 50+** | Hate being sold — "defense is the best offense," "not sponsored," "look into it," blue-collar, never flashy |
| **Men <30** | Pinocchio paradox ("what if it works"), bold claims OK, lifestyle/luxury flex, nonchalant energy |
| **Lead gen (CPL/CPC/CPA)** | Heavy emotion, relatable (not luxury) actors, urgency from a "friend," proof (the check, the bill) |
| **EU / other markets** | Reuse winning US scripts in native language; factual/rational; region-accurate environments |

## Realism markers he holds constant
- **Captions:** white bold centered sans, 1–3 words at a time (TikTok auto-caption). Green for
  educational content. Emoji tally captions for game formats.
- **Skin:** acne, freckles, pores, age lines left in — no smoothing.
- **Lighting:** practical only (window, club neon, lamp, stage wash).
- **Casting:** relatable-not-polished; broad age/ethnicity range.
- **Product:** held loosely or sitting in frame, never shoved at the lens.
- **Locations:** aspirational but believable — ocean-view condos, NYC streets, LA clubs, home
  bedrooms.
- 9:16, high-res (many 4K).

## V4 Realism Laws (technical bible, still governs)
Priority hierarchy: POV correctness → human realism → natural behavior → environmental
authenticity → cinematic quality.
- **POV laws:** the person holding the phone *is* the camera. Rear camera = interviewer holds
  phone, subject on-screen. Front camera = self-confessional/mirror only. Never switch mid-shot.
- **Handheld reality:** one-handed grip, small shakes, thumb adjustments. Forbid gimbal
  smoothness or floating moves.
- **Performance:** speak mid-thought, pause, self-correct, break eye contact. No
  hype/influencer cadence.
- **Audio:** smartphone-mic sound, room tone, environmental bleed. Forbid studio clarity.
  Default = no music.
- **Lighting:** believable sources only (windows, fixtures, lamps, screens).
- **Anti-influencer filter:** subject is not trying to make content — observation, not sales
  energy.
- **Success test:** viewer believes nobody planned or optimized this.

> **This matches and reinforces a lesson this repo already learned the hard way, independently:**
> the front-camera-IS-the-camera POV law above is exactly the fix
> `skills/iphone-selfie-style/SKILL.md` arrived at after two failed attempts (meta camera-position
> instructions don't work; genre-anchoring does) — two separate sources landing on the same rule
> is a good sign it's real, not a one-off quirk of one model.

## Why AI beats human UGC (his pitch)
Full control of tone/emotion/pacing/micro-expressions on command · no waiting weeks on a
creator · consistent persona output · Meta "Andromeda" rewards fresh creative volume · rapid
split-testing of hooks/angles/characters · indistinguishable realism at a fraction of cost.
