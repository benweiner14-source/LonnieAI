## Brand Deal Methodology — How Ben Finds Brands

Ben built a prospecting system based on the Meta Ad Library. Key points:

- **Source:** Meta Ad Library is public and pre-filters for buying intent — everyone in it is actively paying to advertise
- **Filter criteria:** Active video ads (signals creative budget), right niche keywords (recovery, wellness, streetwear, lifestyle), right size (admin-count by country reveals lean founder-led teams vs affiliate farms), legitimate brand website (Shopify etc.), Western-concentrated management
- **Auto-drop criteria:** No real brand site, agency/dropship geo signatures (high non-Western admin counts), too large (15+ admins), known big brands
- **Contact channels:** Privacy policy page emails (legally required contact), LinkedIn for named founders/marketers where company pages exist
- **Key insight:** Admin-count-by-country beats follower count as a lean-team signal — it exposed entire affiliate networks that follower counts missed

This methodology is documented in `methodology.md` (included in repo if available).
